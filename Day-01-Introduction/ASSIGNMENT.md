## Task 3 — Write One LinkedIn Post

Public commitment is part of the deal. Sharing progress is literally point #1 of the 10% Commitment.

- [ ] Publish **one post** on LinkedIn announcing that you're starting this journey.

### What to Include

- What you're starting and why — 26 weeks, 7 tracks, one language
- Where you are right now (complete beginner? some HTML/CSS? switching careers?)
- What you'll have shipped by the end: a full-stack platform, a mobile app, a desktop app, a Chrome extension, an automation system, and an AI assistant
- A link to your **GitHub profile** from Task 2
- Your commitment: 6 hours/week for 6 months, in public

### Rules

- Write it in **your own voice** — no AI-generated filler, people can tell.
- Keep it honest. "I'm a beginner and that's fine" is a stronger post than pretending to be an expert.
- Add a screenshot of your setup or your GitHub profile.

**✅ Deliverable:** the link to your published LinkedIn post.
